Monuments of India

Taj Mahal, Agra

  • Location: Agra, Uttar Pradesh
  • Built: 1632 – 1653 (approx. 21 years)
  • Commissioned by: Emperor Shah Jahan

Red Fort, Delhi

  • Location: New Delhi
  • Built: 12 May 1639 – 6 April 1648; 378 years
  • Built for Mughal Empire

Gateway of India, Mumbai

  • Location: Mumbai, Maharashtra
  • Built: Completed in 1924
  • Built to commemorate King George V's visit

Qutub Minar, Delhi

  • Location: Mehrauli, New Delhi
  • Built: Started in 1199 by Qutb-ud-din Aibak
  • Height: 72.5 m (Tallest brick minaret)

Lotus Temple, Delhi

  • Location: New Delhi
  • Built: Dedicated in December 1986
  • Significance: Bahá'í House of Worship

Stone Chariot, Hampi

  • Location: Vijayanagara, Karnataka
  • Built: 16th Century (Vijayanagara Empire)
  • Shrine dedicated to Garuda inside Vittala Temple
